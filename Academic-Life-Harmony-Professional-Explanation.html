Professional Practice Innovation

Academic-Life Harmony

A Student Self-Management and Growth Observation System

Pareja Integrated School • Structured learner support through goal setting, observation, feedback, reflection, and continuous improvement.

S

Situation

Students manage multiple academic responsibilities, group activities, digital communication, deadlines, and daily classroom demands. I recognized the need for a structured approach that would help learners develop self-management, workload pacing, responsible collaboration, focused learning, help-seeking, and healthy routines rather than simply reacting when difficulties arise.

T

Task

I developed the Academic-Life Harmony framework to provide a practical, student-centered way of identifying one manageable behavior at a time and supporting learners through a recurring cycle of goal setting, observation, feedback, reflection, and improvement.

A

Action

The system uses a monthly rotation:

  • 1. Set a TargetEach learner selects one specific habit to develop.
  • 2. ObserveThe teacher observes learners through a rotating schedule and records concrete behavioral evidence.
  • 3. ReflectLearners receive feedback and compare the observation with their own perception of progress.
  • 4. Adjust & GrowLearners demonstrating consistency move to a new target, while others continue developing the same behavior.
Target→ Observe→ Reflect→ Adjust→ Grow

The teacher observation checklist provides concrete indicators related to workload and time management, boundary-setting, focused transitions, proactive help-seeking, and self-care/ energy preservation.

R

Result & Professional Value

The framework turns classroom observation into a growth-oriented process rather than a one-time evaluation. It provides a systematic way to gather authentic behavioral evidence, give learners actionable feedback, and encourage gradual development of independent habits. The rotation model also makes the process manageable by distributing observations across student groups.

Why This Matters

Academic-Life Harmony demonstrates my approach to classroom management: supporting learners not only in academic performance, but also in developing the habits and self-management skills that enable them to learn effectively and sustain productive routines.

Observe → Understand → Support → Reflect → Improve
