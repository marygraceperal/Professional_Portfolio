EVIDENCE RULES

Before August 31

1. Never claim an outcome that you cannot document. ARAL and Classroom Behaviour Intervention should describe their actual implementation stage until learner outcome evidence becomes available.
2. Completed means actually completed. The August 27 GitHub session and LAC Certificate System should remain "Scheduled" until the activities actually occur.
3. Keep sensitive learner information private. Do not place personally identifiable student information, confidential records or sensitive learner data in your public evidence folders.
4. Curate your evidence. An NZ hiring panel does not need to see everything you have ever produced. Strong, clearly labelled evidence is more useful than a huge archive.
5. August 31 checkpoint. Before beginning your main application push, review the CV, teaching philosophy, cover letter, references, public evidence, intervention documentation and August 27 activities.
