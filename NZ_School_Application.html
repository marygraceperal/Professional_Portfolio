06 · Evidence-Informed Practice

From Idea → Implementation → Evidence → Reflection

1. Identify

Identify learner, classroom or school needs through observation, assessment and professional reflection.

2. Design

Develop an intervention, resource or system designed to address the identified need.

3. Implement

Put the strategy into practice and collect appropriate implementation evidence.

4. Evaluate

Examine outcomes and use evidence to determine what worked and what should be improved.
