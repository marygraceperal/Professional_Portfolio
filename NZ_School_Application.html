Teaching Philosophy

I view mathematics teaching as more than the transmission of mathematical procedures. Effective mathematics education requires learners to develop understanding, confidence, problem-solving ability, and the capacity to apply mathematical ideas in meaningful contexts.

My professional practice therefore combines structured mathematics instruction, targeted learner support, digital learning resources, progress monitoring, collaboration and continuous professional learning.

I am particularly interested in using technology purposefully—not simply because it is available, but because it can make learning resources more accessible, organized and responsive to learner needs.
